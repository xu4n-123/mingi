## 中文

### 明易是什么？

明易是一个中英文双语网站，介绍《周易》与六爻，并提供 AI 辅助的卦象解读。

“明易”的“明”，是理解、看清的意思。我们希望把传统术语解释清楚，让没有相关背景的人也能了解易在说什么，以及它如何把现实问题中的人物、关系和变化放进一套传统的哲学与象征模型中。

网站既介绍文化背景，也让用户亲手体验起卦和阅读卦盘。解读供参考，不替代事实依据、专业意见或个人判断。

### 可以做什么？

- **了解背景**：阅读《周易》与六爻的入门介绍。
- **体验起卦**：使用虚拟铜钱，或手动输入六个爻。
- **阅读解读**：查看本卦、动爻及有动爻时形成的变卦，以及 AI 辅助解读。
- **继续追问**：围绕同一卦盘提出后续问题。
- **回看记录**：登录账号后查看已保存的卦盘与解读。
- **切换语言**：使用中文或英文界面。

### 怎么体验？

1. 打开 [mingi.tech](https://mingi.tech)，选择语言。
2. 可以先阅读介绍；需要起卦时，按页面提示注册、验证邮箱或登录。
3. 写下一个具体问题，选择起卦方式。
4. 阅读解读，或基于同一卦盘继续追问。提问额度以网站显示为准。

请勿在问题或公开反馈中填写真实姓名、联系方式、学校等不必要的个人信息。

## English

### What is Mingi?

Mingi is a bilingual website introducing the *Classic of Changes* and Liuyao, with AI-assisted hexagram readings.

The Chinese name, 明易, combines the idea of understanding with the Changes. Our aim is to explain the terminology and cultural background in plain language, so that readers without prior knowledge can understand what the tradition says. The site also explores how people, relationships and change in a real-life question can be represented within a traditional philosophical and symbolic framework.

Visitors can read an introduction, cast a hexagram and explore its interpretation. Readings are offered as a reference, not as a substitute for evidence, professional advice or personal judgment.

### What can you do?

- **Explore the background:** read introductory material on the Zhou Changes and Liuyao.
- **Cast a hexagram:** use virtual coins or enter six lines manually.
- **Read an interpretation:** view the primary hexagram, changing lines and, where applicable, the relating hexagram, alongside an AI-assisted reading.
- **Ask follow-up questions:** continue exploring the same cast without casting again.
- **Revisit readings:** sign in to access saved casts and interpretations.
- **Switch languages:** use the site in Chinese or English.

### Getting started

1. Visit [mingi.tech](https://mingi.tech) and choose a language.
2. Browse the introduction first, or follow the on-screen steps to register, verify your email or sign in when you are ready to cast.
3. Enter a specific question and choose a casting method.
4. Read the interpretation or ask a follow-up question about the same cast. The website displays your available question allowance.

Avoid including real names, contact details, schools or other unnecessary personal information in questions or public feedback.
