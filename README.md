## 📖 简介 | Introduction

### 什么是六爻？ | What Is Liuyao Divination?

六爻是中国传统《周易》占筮体系的重要组成部分，以六条阴阳爻构成卦象，通过爻的组合与变化进行象征性的推演与解释。

**Liuyao (六爻)**, or Six-Line Divination, is an important part of traditional Chinese *I Ching (Zhou Yi)* divination. It uses six Yin and Yang lines to form hexagrams, interpreting their combinations and transformations through a structured symbolic system.

在《周易》的六十四卦体系中，每个卦象由六条爻自下而上排列而成，其中连续的一横为阳爻（⚊），中间断开的两横为阴爻（⚋）。六爻也可以分为上下两个三爻卦，即八卦中的两个卦象。

Within the *I Ching*'s system of 64 hexagrams, each hexagram consists of six lines arranged from bottom to top. An unbroken line (⚊) represents Yang, while a broken line (⚋) represents Yin. Each hexagram can also be divided into two trigrams, drawn from the Eight Trigrams (*Bagua*).

### 六十四卦 | The 64 Hexagrams

阴阳两种基本符号经过六次组合，可以形成 **2⁶ = 64** 种不同的卦象，即《周易》六十四卦。每一卦都有独特的名称、卦辞及象征意义，与自然现象、人类活动和生活境遇相联系。

Combining two fundamental symbols, Yin and Yang, across six positions produces **2⁶ = 64** possible configurations, known as the 64 hexagrams of the *I Ching*. Each hexagram has its own name, classical judgment, and symbolic associations, drawing upon natural phenomena, human experiences, and changing circumstances.

例如，乾卦（䷀）由六条阳爻组成，常以天为象，象征刚健与创造；坤卦（䷁）由六条阴爻组成，以地为象，体现柔顺与承载。其他卦象则通过不同的阴阳组合，呈现雷、水、山、风等自然意象，以及与人类处境有关的丰富象征。

For example, **Qian (乾, ䷀)** consists of six Yang lines and is associated with Heaven, strength, and creative power, while **Kun (坤, ䷁)** consists of six Yin lines and is associated with Earth, receptivity, and nurturing. Other hexagrams evoke imagery such as thunder, water, mountains, and wind, alongside symbolic interpretations of human situations.

**明易为卦象设计了形象化的视觉插画，将传统卦象的自然意象、象征意义与数字艺术相结合。** 我们希望用户不仅能够辨认六条阴阳爻，还能够通过画面直观感受卦象所承载的文化联想，让抽象符号成为可以观看、探索与理解的视觉语言。

**Mingi brings these ancient symbols to life through expressive hexagram illustrations**, combining traditional imagery, symbolic meanings, and digital art. Rather than presenting hexagrams solely as abstract arrangements of lines, we aim to make their cultural associations visually approachable, transforming symbolic structures into an experience that users can see, explore, and interpret.

### 动爻与卦变 | Changing Lines and Hexagram Transformation

在传统筮法中，每条爻可以对应四种数值：

In traditional divination methods, each line can take one of four numerical values:

| 爻值 Value | 中文名称 Chinese Name | English | 阴阳属性 Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6** | 老阴 | Old Yin | 阴爻，动爻 / Changing Yin |
| **7** | 少阳 | Young Yang | 阳爻，静爻 / Unchanging Yang |
| **8** | 少阴 | Young Yin | 阴爻，静爻 / Unchanging Yin |
| **9** | 老阳 | Old Yang | 阳爻，动爻 / Changing Yang |

其中，老阴（6）与老阳（9）被视为动爻。动爻由阴转阳或由阳转阴后，可以形成新的卦象，分别称为**本卦（Primary Hexagram）**与**变卦（Resulting Hexagram）**。

Old Yin (6) and Old Yang (9) are considered *changing lines*. When these lines transform into their opposites, a new hexagram emerges. The original hexagram is called the **Primary Hexagram (本卦)**, while the transformed one is known as the **Resulting Hexagram (变卦)**.

六爻体系不仅体现了阴阳符号的组合规律，也反映了中国传统思想中对变化、关系与情境的持续关注。在后世易学发展中，六爻占法还逐渐融合了五行、干支、六亲及纳甲等更加复杂的解释体系。

The six-line system demonstrates how simple Yin–Yang symbols can form complex patterns of change. It also reflects a longstanding concern in Chinese thought with transformation, relationships, and changing circumstances. Later divination traditions further incorporated the Five Elements, Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, Six Relations, and Najia systems.

Mingi offers two ways to construct a hexagram: virtual coin casting and manual six-line input.

## 🎨 设计理念 | Design Philosophy

### 从复杂符号到直观体验 | From Complex Symbols to Intuitive Experiences

《周易》的六十四卦由简单的阴阳符号构成，却形成了复杂的组合关系与象征体系。明易希望通过交互设计与卦象插画，在严谨的符号结构与直观的视觉体验之间建立联系。

The 64 hexagrams of the *I Ching* emerge from simple Yin and Yang lines, yet form an intricate system of combinations and symbolism. Through interaction design and illustrated imagery, Mingi seeks to connect these structured symbols with intuitive visual experiences.

### 从文化保存到文化理解 | From Cultural Preservation to Cultural Understanding

我们相信，数字化不仅意味着保存或复制传统文化，也可以成为重新理解和探索传统的过程。明易希望用户不仅获得卦象的解释，还能够逐渐发现其背后的文化思想、符号关系与解释方式。

We believe digitization should go beyond preserving or reproducing cultural traditions. It can also create new opportunities for interpretation, exploration, and understanding.
Mingi aims to help users move beyond receiving interpretations toward discovering the cultural ideas, symbolic relationships, and interpretive traditions behind them.

**Our goal is not merely to deliver answers, but to inspire curiosity about the systems and ideas behind them.**

**我们的目标不仅是提供答案，更是激发人们对答案背后的体系与思想的好奇。**
